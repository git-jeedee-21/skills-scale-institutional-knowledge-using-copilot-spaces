# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers translate user needs into usable, accessible, and valuable product experiences. They work with product and engineering partners to ensure the solution meets both customer outcomes and delivery constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Define user journeys, flows, wireframes, and design systems
- Validate accessibility, inclusion, and usability standards
- Translate product goals into design requirements and acceptance criteria
- Provide design feedback during implementation and QA

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by validating assumptions early
- Align product outcomes with user needs and accessibility standards

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and roadmap reviews
- Design critiques, prototypes, and research summaries
- Collaboration with Developers and QA on feasibility and usability validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to validate problem statements, user value, and adoption goals.
- Partner with Developers to ensure designs are feasible, testable, and consistent with technical constraints.
- Support Project Managers by surfacing design dependencies, risks, and timeline impacts.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to validate the user experience against acceptance criteria.

---

## Tech Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects guide technical direction, architecture decisions, and implementation quality. They help teams deliver solutions that are maintainable, secure, and aligned with platform constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, patterns, and platform choices
- Guide solution design and integration decisions
- Review architecture trade-offs and technical risks
- Support engineering estimation, sequencing, and dependency planning
- Ensure system quality, maintainability, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and resilient solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable rework
- Align implementation choices with product and delivery goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and technical design reviews
- Development planning, dependency tracking, and risk escalation
- Collaboration with stakeholders on trade-offs and delivery impacts

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to support implementation quality and technical decision-making.
- Work with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, time, and technical constraints.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies, sequencing, and delivery risks.
- Partner with QA/Testing to define testability requirements and validation strategies for complex integrations.

---

## DevOps and Site Reliability Engineers (SREs)

### Role Summary
DevOps and SRE roles ensure the team can build, deploy, monitor, and operate systems reliably. They support delivery velocity without sacrificing quality, reliability, and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Manage CI/CD pipelines, environments, and release automation
- Support deployment readiness, monitoring, alerts, and rollback plans
- Improve system reliability, performance, and incident response readiness
- Define operational guardrails for production support and recovery
- Partner with engineering on environment consistency and deployment practices

### Goals
- Increase delivery confidence and reduce operational risk
- Improve platform reliability and production readiness
- Enable quick recovery from failures and easier handoff to support teams

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness reviews
- Incident response coordination and postmortems
- Operational dashboards, alerts, and reliability discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers and Tech Leads to support automation, environments, and deployment workflows.
- Work with Project Managers to track release risk, rollback readiness, and deployment windows.
- Support QA/Testing with staging environments and validation of production-like systems.
- Collaborate with Security/Privacy Leads on secure deployment practices and monitoring controls.

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads protect the organization and users by ensuring risks, controls, and compliance obligations are addressed throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements for security, privacy, and compliance needs
- Identify vulnerabilities, threat scenarios, and mitigation strategies
- Validate that sensitive data handling and permissions are appropriate
- Support security reviews, testing, and remediation planning
- Help define acceptable risk levels and response pathways for incidents

### Goals
- Reduce product and operational risk
- Build trust with users, stakeholders, and regulators
- Make security and privacy part of delivery, not a late-stage add-on

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and risk assessments
- Release readiness checks and threat review updates
- Escalations for incidents or high-risk dependencies

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Stakeholders to define security and privacy requirements early in planning.
- Partner with Developers and Tech Leads to review design choices, controls, and secure implementation patterns.
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to validate controls and supporting evidence.
- Support Project Managers in identifying security dependencies, risks, and escalation paths.

---

## Data and Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads ensure the team measures outcomes, understands behavior, and uses evidence to improve value delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define analytics requirements, instrumentation, and metrics tracking
- Support success metric definitions and dashboards
- Validate data quality, reporting accuracy, and measurement relevance
- Align experimentation and measurement plans with product goals
- Help interpret trends and recommend improvements based on evidence

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with measurable evidence
- Ensure adoption, performance, and customer impact are observable
- Connect delivery work to outcome-based success metrics

### Typical Communication
- Product strategy reviews and milestone check-ins
- Dashboard reviews, experiment planning, and outcome measurement updates
- Collaboration with Product and technical teams on instrumentation needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define the right metrics and success criteria.
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to ensure instrumentation is implemented correctly and validated.
- Provide Project Managers with measurable progress signals and risk indicators.
- Coordinate with Stakeholders to communicate results and performance trends in plain language.

---

## Business and Change Managers

### Role Summary
Business and Change Managers help teams and stakeholders adopt changes successfully and minimize disruption during implementation or rollout.

### Responsibilities
- Identify impacted teams, stakeholder groups, and readiness needs
- Define communication plans, training needs, and adoption milestones
- Coordinate change readiness across business and operational stakeholders
- Monitor stakeholder feedback and adoption barriers
- Support rollout and transition activities for process or product change

### Goals
- Increase adoption and reduce friction during change
- Make project outcomes sustainable beyond delivery
- Ensure stakeholders are prepared and aligned for new ways of working

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, readiness check-ins, and rollout updates
- Training and communication plans for impacted groups
- Post-launch adoption and feedback discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on value communication and stakeholder impact.
- Collaborate with Project Managers to align readiness plans with release timing and dependencies.
- Support Developers and QA/Testing by clarifying business workflows and user adoption scenarios.
- Coordinate with Security and other operational teams when rollout impacts governance or support practices.

---

## Role Combination Guidance for Smaller Teams

On smaller projects, one person may hold multiple roles. In those cases, the team should still document ownership, decision boundaries, and escalation paths clearly so accountability is preserved even when roles are combined. For example, a Developer may also act as a Tech Lead, or a Product Manager may also own stakeholder communication and change management tasks.
